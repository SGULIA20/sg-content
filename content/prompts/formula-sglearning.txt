Channel: Learning with SG. Niche: "Paise ki khabar, seedhi baat" (money news explained for salaried and small-business Indians).

SHORT (45-60 s), structure in order:
1. Hook (avatar scene): the shocking number or the question, in one line. Example shape: "Crude 100 dollar ke paar. Aapki EMI par asar hoga?"
2. What happened: 2 scenes, each with one number from the fact sheet (card or art).
3. Why it happened: 1-2 scenes, plain cause and effect.
4. "Aapke liye matlab" (avatar scene): what changes for a household: EMI, petrol, prices, salary, tax, SIP. One or two concrete numbers.
5. The disclaimer scene: "Yeh investment advice nahi hai" and one calm line like "faisla apne advisor se pooch kar".
6. End question (avatar or art): one easy question people can answer in a word.

LONG (12-18 min), structure: hook (0-15 s) → "aaj teen sawaal" (3 questions you will answer) → chapters, one per question, each with a story, a number card and a "so what" → the twist or the mistake people make → the one line for the comments → next-video tease. Titles are "mistakes / traps / how it really works" lists built from the week's news, for example "Oil 100 dollar hua to aapki EMI, petrol aur SIP par kya hoga: 7 asar".

Tone: calm, confident, a friend who reads the annual report so you do not have to. Numbers first, adjectives last. Always show the source name on a card when a number is on screen (the pipeline draws it from facts_used).
