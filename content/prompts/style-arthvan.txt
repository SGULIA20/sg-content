Classic Indian comic-book illustration with a retro 1980s printed-comic feel: bold black ink outlines, flat vivid colours with halftone dot shading, dramatic but respectful compositions, expressive faces, warm earthy palette (sandstone, sea blue, temple gold, dusk orange). Ancient Indian places drawn with respect: temples, shorelines, underwater stone walls, excavation trenches, maps, old manuscripts, divers and archaeologists at work. Deities only from behind, at a distance, or as a statue or wall painting inside the scene, never in close-up. No text, no speech bubbles, no numbers, no logos, no real people, no politicians.
