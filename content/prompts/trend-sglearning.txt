
VIRAL SUBJECT (founder, 24 Nov 2026: "why can't Claude search viral reels and make the same kind of reels irrespective of chosen topic ... any topic for sglearning"). This video (Short or long) is about a subject the world is talking about right now; it may come from any country. Learning with SG is a channel to learn anything, not only money. These rules REPLACE the money rules above wherever they differ:
- The subject need not be about money. Do not force a money, EMI, SIP or salary angle onto it. Tell what happened, why, and the one thing most viewers have got wrong or do not know.
- Same form and length as the rules above give for this kind of video (Short or long): plain words a 15-year-old follows, short sentences. A long video keeps its chapters, each ending on the next question.
- Scene 1 opens on the strangest true fact of the subject in short beats (two to seven words a sentence), then one question. The viewer must recognise the subject in the first three words.
- Every second scene ends on a small hook for the next one. A reveal comes in the last third, not the first.
- Do NOT speak the investment disclaimer and do not put it in the caption: this is not a money video.
- Only facts from the fact sheet. Real people: only what the sheet reports, said as reported ("reportedly", "court ne kaha"). Never a rumour, never mockery, no party politics, no communal angle.
- Think worldwide (founder, 24 Nov 2026): tell it so a Hindi-speaking viewer in any country follows it. Give a foreign place, person or amount one plain line of context the first time; money in its own currency with the rupee figure beside it only when the fact sheet gives one. Do not turn every story back to India.
- Never copy another creator's video, wording or title: our own telling of the subject.
- The send line names who to send it to, for THIS subject.

TRAGEDIES AND DISASTERS (24 Nov 2026): when the subject is a disaster, an accident, a crime or a death, the telling is sober and respectful. Explain what happened and why, and what changed afterwards; name the dead only as the sources do, with numbers given as reported. No cliff-hanger built on someone's death, no "aap hairaan reh jayenge", no jokes, no shock emoji in the title or caption, no graphic picture (show the place, the documents, the memorial, people from behind). Never tie it to a different, recent event or guess at a cause the fact sheet does not give. The founder's opening line states the question plainly; it does not sensationalise.
