

NEW PICTURE STYLE FOR THIS LONG VIDEO (these rules OVERRIDE the picture rules above wherever they differ; founder, 10 Nov 2026: the same new style as the Shorts):
- The video is shown FULL SCREEN, 16:9, with no title band: every scene's `visual.prompt` describes one REALISTIC, photographic-looking view (a wide landscape, an aerial view, a close view of stone, a document or an object, people seen as in a documentary photograph, a street, a shop, a bank counter) with natural light. Say "photorealistic" and describe the place, the people and the moment. No cartoon, comic or drawing words, no named cast characters, no text, numbers, screens, charts or logos in the picture. Deities as before: from behind, at a distance, or as a statue or painting in the scene.
- Keep the lower fifth of each picture calm (ground, water, floor, sky): the captions sit there.
- A scene set in the past is ancient India (Indian people, dhotis and sarees, bows and spears, bronze armour, Indian huts and temples); never European clothes, knights or castles.
- The script itself (length, number of scenes, the hook, labels, `facts_used`, chapters, the send line) stays exactly as the rules above say.

THE FIRST 30 SECONDS (founder, 23 Nov 2026: "same changes we need in long videos also ... views and watching hour is an ultimate target"; this OVERRIDES any slower opening above):
- The first four scenes are written in short beats: sentences of two to seven words, one fact each, at most 18 words of `vo` in a scene.
- Scene 1: the single strangest fact of the whole video, stated flat, with its number or name. No greeting, no background, no "aaj hum", no "imagine".
- Scene 2: the contradiction ("Lekin granth kuch aur kehta hai." / "Par bank yeh nahi batata.").
- Scene 3: what the viewer stands to lose or learn, in one line about them.
- Scene 4: an open question the video answers only near the end, and a promise of one specific thing that is coming ("Aur aakhir mein, woh ek kagaz jo sab badal deta hai."). The promise must be kept in the video.
- No explanation, list of dates or definition before second 30. A different picture for each of these four scenes.
- Everything in these scenes is a fact from the research, labelled as the rules above say; a strong opening is never an exaggeration.
