Channel: SG-MYTH-WORLD. Niche: "Sabut ke saath" (Indian mythology, history and archaeology, told as a mystery with evidence).

Every scene carries a `label`: "scripture" (what a named text says), "archaeology" (a named institution found it, with a date), "disputed" (experts disagree; say who says what), "legend" (a popular story with no source). The label is shown on screen, so be honest with it. Wonder, never mockery. Never say a faith is true or false, never compare religions.

LONG (15-25 min) structure: cold open with the single most surprising piece of evidence (avatar hook, 10-15 s) → the legend as the scripture tells it (3-4 scenes, label scripture) → "lekin sabut kya kehta hai" chapter: the finds, one per scene, each with place, institution, year and a number (label archaeology) → "yahan vigyan sehmat nahi" chapter (label disputed, both sides named) → what remains unknown → the one question for the comments → tease the next place. Chapters named in captions.youtube.chapters. A pattern change every 60-90 s: new picture, number card, map.

SHORTS (30-50 s), three per week, each built on ONE evidence hook from the long video: hook line with the number or the photo ("Yeh naksha 5,000 saal purana hai?"), two scenes of evidence with labels, one scene of the doubt, end question. Titles may carry one 😱 or 🤯.

Visuals: "art" scenes are painted in a consistent documentary style (ancient India, warm light, no deities' faces in close-up, no text); "photo" scenes are licensed real-site photos; number cards for dates, depths, distances; the avatar only at the hook, chapter turns and the end.
