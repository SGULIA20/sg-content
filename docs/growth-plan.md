# Growth plan: YouTube income (founder, 30 Oct 2026)

Goal: YouTube income. That means the YouTube Partner Program (ads share): 1,000 subscribers, plus either 4,000 public watch
hours on long videos in 12 months or 10 million Shorts views in 90 days. The two routes cannot be combined. Each channel
qualifies on its own, and YouTube reviews it (about a month). Check the exact numbers on each channel's Studio "Earn" page.

- 4,000 hours is about 4,600 minutes a week, so SG-MYTH-WORLD needs about 8x, and Learning with SG about 50x.
- The Shorts route (10 million in 90 days, about 111,000 views a day) is out of reach for now. We get about 150 a day.
- So the realistic route is **long-video watch time, starting with SG-MYTH-WORLD**, plus moving the 17,000 Instagram
  followers over to YouTube.

## Free levers (running)

1. **Big questions, never the day's index move.** On 22-28 Oct, curiosity questions (Mahabharat date, Harappan DNA,
   US stocks from $1) got 1,300-1,580 views. The day's Sensex or Nifty move got 28-42. Topic scoring now rejects index-move
   headlines, and daily US market wraps are skipped.
2. **Hooks.** Since 25 Oct every Reel opens on the claim or the question, with a number or a name, in at most 12 words,
   and never on a scene. Measure: the early-skip rate in the Monday report should fall from about 45% to under 30%.
3. **Shorts to long videos.** Each Short already links its long video (description and pinned comment).
4. **Instagram to YouTube (founder, 2 minutes).** Put the YouTube channel link first in both Instagram bios. Once a week,
   share the new long video as a Story with a link sticker. Our app cannot add link stickers, so this is done in the app.
5. **Collab posts** (draft below). One post appears on both accounts, at no cost.

## The Rs 6,000 test: spend it on YouTube, not Instagram

An Instagram boost buys Instagram views, and Instagram views pay nothing on YouTube. Run the test from YouTube Studio
(Promote) or Google Ads, on the best long video of each channel:

- Goal: **subscribers**. Watch time bought with ads is not expected to count toward the 4,000 public hours. Check YouTube's YPP
  help page before relying on it. Subscribers do count, and they bring free watch time afterwards.
- Budget: Rs 3,000 per channel over 14 days. Audience: India, Hindi, 18-44.
- Record: subscribers gained, cost per subscriber, and the next week's free (non-ad) minutes watched.
- Scale to Rs 15,000 a month only if a subscriber costs about Rs 15 or less **and** free minutes watched rise at least 30%.

## Collab outreach (Instagram Collab post) - draft for the founder to send

Who to ask: Hindi finance creators (for Learning with SG) or Hindi history and mythology creators (for SG-MYTH-WORLD) with
20,000-300,000 followers. Look for real comments, not giveaways, and nothing that could look like stock tips. Five to eight
messages a week.

> Namaste [name] ji, main Sanjeev Gulia (FCMA), Learning with SG chalata hoon (17k on Instagram). Aapki [specific Reel]
> bahut achhi lagi, khaas kar [one detail]. Ek idea: ek Collab Reel saath mein, "[topic both audiences care about]".
> Script aur edit hum kar denge, aap sirf review karke Collab accept karein; post dono profiles par jaayega. Agar theek lage
> toh main 2 topic options bhej deta hoon?

Good shared topics: "Gold ya SIP: 10 saal ka asli hisaab", "Purane zamane ka paisa: Harappa ka trade". Never stock or fund
picks, never a "guaranteed return", and no paid deal without a written note.
